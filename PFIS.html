System Overview

The Predictive Fraud Intelligence System (PFIS) is an AI-powered fraud prevention platform commissioned by the Financial Intelligence Centre. It is designed to monitor and evaluate financial transactions in real time across Ghana's financial infrastructure.

Unlike conventional fraud systems that respond reactively after fraudulent activity has occurred, PFIS employs a proactive, predictive approach. By continuously learning the behavioural patterns of each user, PFIS can identify subtle deviations that may signal manipulation or unauthorised access — often before the customer even realises they are being targeted.

National Mandate: In accordance with regulatory directive FIC/2024/001, all licensed financial institutions operating in Ghana are required to integrate with PFIS by Q3 2025.

How PFIS Works

1
Transaction Entry
Every transaction — from banking apps, mobile money services, or online platforms — passes through PFIS before being processed by the financial institution.
2
Data Collection
PFIS collects transaction metadata: device ID, amount, timestamp, recipient, geolocation, and SIM card integrity status.
3
Hurdle Evaluation
The transaction passes through six verification hurdles, each testing a different dimension of the user's established behavioural baseline.
4
Risk Scoring (0–100)
A composite risk score is calculated. Scores 0–40 are safe; 41–70 are monitored; 71–100 are blocked and alerted.
5
Dual Alert System
For high-risk transactions, the customer receives an immediate alert and the fraud investigation team is simultaneously notified.
6
Continuous Learning
PFIS refines its models by analysing confirmed fraud cases, adapting thresholds, and incorporating new fraud patterns identified by the FIC.

The Multi-Hurdle Risk Framework

HurdleWeightDescription
Device Identity20 ptsChecks whether the initiating device matches the user's known registered devices.
Amount vs. Baseline25 ptsCompares transaction amount against the user's historical average and typical range.
Transaction Timing15 ptsEvaluates whether the transaction occurs within the user's normal activity hours.
Known Location15 ptsVerifies that the originating geolocation is consistent with the user's known locations.
Known Recipient15 ptsChecks if the recipient account is one the user has previously transacted with.
SIM Integrity10 ptsVerifies that the user's SIM card has not been recently swapped — a key indicator of SIM-swap fraud.

Legal & Regulatory Framework

PFIS operates within the following legislative and regulatory instruments:

  • Financial Intelligence Centre Act, 2013 (Act XYZ)
  • Anti-Money Laundering Act, 2020 (Act 1044)
  • Payment Systems and Services Act, 2019 (Act 987)
  • Electronic Transactions Act, 2008 (Act 772)
  • Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)
  • FIC Directive FIC/2024/001 — Real-Time Fraud Monitoring
All data collected by PFIS is processed in accordance with Ghana's Data Protection Act. User data is encrypted, access-controlled, and subject to the data retention policies of the Financial Intelligence Centre.
